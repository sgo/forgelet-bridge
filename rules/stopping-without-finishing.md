## Stopping Without Finishing

- Work you hold has three honest endings: hand it off, finish it, or ask.
- If you stop while a card is assigned to you and you are not handing it off, raise a clarification first — `pack_dashboard_request.sh clarify ./tmp/question.txt` — naming the card, what stops you, what you already tried, and what would let you continue. Then stop and wait.
- A question you can answer yourself is not a clarification; continue instead.
- Do not start unrelated work to fill the time, and do not go quiet: a role that stops silently is indistinguishable from a role that is stuck, and neither the operator nor the forge can tell a stall from a quiet one.
- Idle with nothing assigned is not a stall: report `NO_TASK` and wait.
