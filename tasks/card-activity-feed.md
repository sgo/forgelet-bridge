# card-activity-feed

As cards move through a project I only see it if I go looking. I want short
updates on my phone as they change lanes — a card appearing, moving on to the
next role, finishing — saying the project, the card, and where it moved. I will
not act on these, so they belong somewhere I can keep quiet and read as a log,
not in the room where approvals wait. What I am really after is the silence
between them: if nothing arrives for a long stretch while work is supposedly in
flight, that is my signal that an agent may be stuck, so I do not want a regular
heartbeat — an update should mean something actually changed.
