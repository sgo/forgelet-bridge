# post-restart-encryption

Today the restart scenarios only prove the bridge does not duplicate what it
already delivered. I want the stronger thing proven: after the bridge is
stopped and started again, it is still the same Matrix device, and the next
answer still reaches me as a message I can read on my phone. If restarting can
leave the bridge unable to send readable messages, or leave me looking at a new
unverified device every time it restarts, I want the acceptance suite to catch
that instead of me noticing silence. The rest of the chat slice stays as it is.
